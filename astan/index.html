What's your net worth, really?

Business owners don't typically track their true net worth. Rare Company makes this a formal part of our membership, so you know exactly where you stand - and how to grow yours:

  • Money - what the company actually pays you, and what you've built outside it.
  • Assets - what the business would be worth without you standing in it.
  • Time - the hours you keep, the evenings back, the holidays that survive.
  • Opportunity - the choices genuinely open to you: grow, acquire, step back, sell.
  • State - whether you can switch off, and whether you still enjoy owning it.

We measure your net worth throughout the membership, so you can hold us to account for your ROI.

Asked, answered.

What does it cost?

A single fixed monthly fee, on a twelve-month term - usually treated as a business expense. There's nothing else to buy, and nothing taken from anything you ever sell. The full detail is on the membership page.

What exactly is a Circle?

A private group of six to eight established owners at a similar stage, meeting in person in your city every month, chaired by people who have built, grown and sold companies. Same faces each time; commitments recorded and followed up.

Who is it for?

Established British owners and directors serious about making their business the exception - properly profitable, easier to run, worth more - whether a sale is five years away, further, or not the plan at all.

Will anyone try to sell to me?

No. Nobody is admitted to prospect the membership. It's the first house rule, and the most protected one.

Is it genuinely confidential?

What your company is worth - and whether you might one day sell it - is about the most commercially sensitive information there is, so nothing leaves the room. No notes, no quotes, no exceptions.

How are Circles matched?

By stage, scale, intent and personality - never with a direct competitor. The aim is a table where someone has already faced whatever is coming next for you.

How much time does it take?

One Circle session a month, events as you choose, and the network when you need it. Membership should give you time back, not consume it - that's half the point.

What if it's not right for me?

The first ninety days are protected: your fees come back in full. After that, membership must earn its place every year at your annual review - and if it hasn't, we say so first.
