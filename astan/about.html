About us

Why we built Rare Company.

In our words

A letter from the founders.

Hello,

We're Eddie and Astan. Between us we've built, scaled and sold companies to the tune of over £20 million - Eddie's software business went to a NASDAQ-listed buyer in under four years; Astan has exited several times at seven and eight figures.

Here's the honest bit: we got there in spite of how we ran things, not because of it. For years our companies ran on us. We worked the hours, carried every decision alone, and paid ourselves last. Every lesson was learned the expensive way, with our own money and our own time.

The people who could have shortened all of that - owners a few years ahead, founders who had already sold - were exactly the people we never sat with. We made it anyway. It just took longer, cost more and swallowed more of our lives than it needed to.

So after our exits, we built the room we went looking for and never found. A Circle of six to eight vetted owners in your city, in person every month, backed by a national network of founders who have done it. Nothing is off the table: pricing, acquisitions, hiring a proper number two, taking money out, the things you can't say to your team or your family.

Two rules keep it that way. Not everyone who applies is accepted - we would rather leave a seat empty than give it to the wrong person. And strictly no selling: nobody is in the room to pitch you, ever.

One more promise, while we have you: we will tell you the truth - about your business, your timing, and whether we can help - even when it costs us a member.

If you've built something real and it still owns too much of your life, apply. The conversation costs nothing.

Eddie Eddie Whittingham Co-founder Sold his software company to a NASDAQ-listed buyer
Astan Astan Morarji Co-founder Multiple seven and eight-figure exits
