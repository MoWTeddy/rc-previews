Part one

What membership includes.

One fee, one goal: a rare business that pays you more, needs you less and gives you your life back. Built around your Circle, in your city - three circles, one membership.

The first circle

Your Circle

Monthly · In your city

This Circle will change how your company runs - then it starts on the rest of your life.

The wealthiest owners have always had a room like this. Most never find it - they make every big call alone. Yours holds six to eight established owners in your city: the closest thing an owner gets to colleagues.

What happens in a session

Three hours, in person, chaired by a founder who has built and sold. Real numbers on the table, one member's decision taken apart properly, and every commitment recorded - then checked at the next session. Same faces every month, so nobody starts from scratch.

How we build one

Every seat is interviewed, vetted and there by application - never a direct competitor. We match a ladder, not a mirror: peers at your level, owners a few years ahead, and founders who have finished the journey - so someone at the table has already faced what's coming next for you.

House rules

How the membership is run.

  1. i.In confidence

    What is said in the room stays in the room. No notes, no quotes, no exceptions.

  2. ii.Experience, not theory

    Every table is anchored by people who have done the thing, not studied it, advised on it or posted about it.

  3. iii.No pitching

    Nothing is sold in the room. Not services, not deals, not yourself. Nobody here wants your money.

  4. iv.By name

    There are no open tickets. Every seat is considered, and every member is there by name.

  5. v.Rare settings

    Private dining rooms, townhouses and tables you cannot book. The setting is part of the conversation.
