Questions

Asked, answered.

What does it cost?

£1,000 a month, on a twelve-month term - typically treated as a business expense. No joining fee, no success fee, and no percentage of anything you ever sell.

What exactly is a Circle?

A private group of six to eight established owners at a similar stage, meeting in person in your city every month, chaired by people who have built, grown and sold companies. Same faces each time; commitments recorded and followed up.

Who is it for?

Established British owners and directors serious about making their business the exception - properly profitable, easier to run, worth more - whether a sale is five years away, further, or not the plan at all.

Will anyone try to sell to me?

No. Nobody is admitted to prospect the room - no advisers hunting clients, no introducers, no sponsors. It is the first house rule and the most protected.

Is it genuinely confidential?

What your company is worth - and whether you might one day sell it - is about the most commercially sensitive information there is. Confidentiality isn't a feature; it's the precondition. No notes, no quotes, no exceptions.

How are Circles matched?

By stage, scale, intent and personality - never with a direct competitor. The aim is a table where someone has already faced whatever is coming next for you.

How much time does it take?

One Circle session a month, events as you choose, and the network when you need it. Membership should give you time back, not consume it - that's half the point.

What if it's not right for me?

The first ninety days are protected: your fees come back in full. After that, membership must earn its place every year at your annual review - and if it hasn't, we say so first.
