Membership

What you get for £1,000 a month.

The problem

Why businesses stay common.

Not for lack of effort. Staying common is the natural outcome unless something interrupts it - and six things keep it that way:

  1. i.The owner is trapped inside what they built

    Every problem gets solved personally. The company grows around their capability instead of beyond it.

  2. ii.Activity gets mistaken for progress

    More customers, more staff, more revenue - more complexity, and no more reward for the owner.

  3. iii.The advice arrives in fragments

    The accountant sees tax. The lawyer sees risk. The coach sees mindset. Nobody looks at the whole ownership equation.

  4. iv.Everyone at the table needs something

    Staff, suppliers, advisers, customers - all with their own interests. Almost nobody is focused on making ownership pay.

  5. v.Common is the benchmark

    Measured against other common businesses, thin margins and heavy workloads look inevitable.

  6. vi.The owners who solved it are out of reach

    The people who built profitable, owner-independent companies are exactly the people most owners never meet.

Staying common isn't a lack of ambition. It's what happens when nothing interrupts it. Membership is the interruption.

Part one

What membership includes.

One fee, one goal: a rare business that pays you more, needs you less and gives you your life back. Built around your Circle, in your city - five parts, one membership.

No. 1

Your Circle

The same trusted owners, every month, in your city.

Monthly · In your city

A private group of established owners at a similar stage, scale and level of ambition - meeting every month in your city, chaired and supported by people who have built, grown and sold rare companies.

The same faces return each month, so they know your company, your numbers, your goals and the decisions in front of you. This isn't networking. It's the closest thing an owner gets to colleagues: people who challenge your thinking, share what worked for them, and tell you what nobody around you will.

Every Circle is matched deliberately - by scale, stage and intent, never with direct competitors - and every seat is an established owner, there by application. The matching philosophy is a ladder, not a mirror: peers at your stage, owners a few years ahead, and founders who have finished the journey - so someone at the table has already faced whatever is coming next for you.

Three things are non-negotiable in every Circle: commitment (you turn up, every month), candour (real numbers, real problems), and confidentiality (what is said in the room stays in the room).

No. 2

The Network

The whole membership, on demand, between meetings.

Whenever you need it

A buyer approaches. A senior hire goes wrong. An adviser pushes a course of action. Between Circle meetings, send us the question and we connect you with members who have already faced it - within a day, not at next month's meeting.

Behind that sits real experience across the things that change a company: growth, operations, finance, tax, legal, exit. Bring the issue; we bring the right people.

And when you do need to hire help, the membership will tell you which advisers are actually worth it - recommended by members, vetted by us, and rated by owners who have paid them. No paid listings, ever.

No. 3

Events

Private dinners and conversations across the UK.

Monthly · Across the UK

Private dinners, breakfasts and events with serious owners, exited founders and trusted operators - across the UK, every month.

No badges, no speeches, no rooms full of people selling to each other. Every table is composed: owners at similar stages, people with genuine context and no agenda.

The conversations that change businesses happen over dinner, not at conferences - and the insight rarely goes public: what actually grew other owners' companies, what buyers are really paying, which advisers deliver.

No. 4

Experiences

Uncommon occasions you can't book your way into.

Quarterly

Every quarter, something genuinely rare: sporting occasions, cultural visits, overseas trips - with your Circle and the wider membership.

They exist because trust is built in hours, not agenda items. The relationships made here are the ones you will draw on for years - and the settings are part of the conversation: private rooms and tables you cannot book.

No. 5

Your Rare Company

Your value, your plan, your progress - tracked from day one.

From day one · Reviewed throughout

Membership begins with a private assessment of your company: what it's worth today, how much of that depends on you, and what would make the biggest difference to your money, your time and your freedom.

That becomes your plan, and your number. Both are revisited as the company changes and formally reviewed through the year - so you always know what your company is worth, and what membership has been worth.

House rules

How the membership is run.

  1. i.In confidence

    What is said in the room stays in the room. No notes, no quotes, no exceptions.

  2. ii.Experience, not theory

    Every table is anchored by people who have done the thing, not studied it, advised on it or posted about it.

  3. iii.No pitching

    Nothing is sold in the room. Not services, not deals, not yourself. Nobody here wants your money.

  4. iv.By name

    There are no open tickets. Every seat is considered, and every member is there by name.

  5. v.Rare settings

    Private dining rooms, townhouses and tables you cannot book. The setting is part of the conversation.

Part three

Who it's for.

Owners and leaders of established UK businesses who refuse to let what they've built stay common - some selling within five years, some maybe never, all wanting the choice. Plus a few exited founders, who sit at the table for the company, not for a fee.

We keep the membership deliberately small: owners at your stage, owners years ahead, and founders who've already sold - never advisers, brokers, or anyone who would treat the room as a pipeline. Nothing is sold inside Rare Company, and nobody here wants your money.

Membership carries expectations as well as benefits: you turn up for your Circle, you speak candidly, and you contribute your experience as readily as you draw on everyone else's. The room only works because everyone in it makes it work.
