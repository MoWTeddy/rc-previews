Not every successful business owner belongs here.

Some have built a good company and are perfectly happy keeping it that way.

Rare Company is for those who want a lot more.

Owners who are still pushing to grow, acquire, build proper leadership, increase the value of their company or prepare for a significant exit.

We carefully select every member, because the quality of the people around the table determines the quality of the membership.

Our members are typically at £2m-£20m turnover (we have a minimum £1m annual turnover or significant cash exit entry requirement).

But ambition matters far more than the number you're doing today.
