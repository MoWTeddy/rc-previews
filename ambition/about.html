We were fed up with being the most experienced people in the room.

We tried the networking groups, the peer groups and the overhyped “masterminds”. The breakfasts. The memberships. The rooms full of people calling themselves advisers, mentors and experts.

Too often, they were filled with people looking for clients rather than peers. People with little to teach. People who had never built, owned or sold anything meaningful, confidently advising those who had.

The conversation stayed polite. The advice stayed obvious. The real numbers never came out. And somehow, the person running the group was often the person you would least want running your business.

For established owners, that is not just disappointing. It is useless.

When the decision concerns millions of pounds, your family's future or years of your life, you cannot afford generic advice from people without the experience, judgement or scars to back it up.

You need owners worth listening to. People who have made serious money, lost it, rebuilt, hired badly, bought companies, taken cash out, stepped away and sold. People who understand the pressure because they have carried it themselves.

That was the group we kept looking for. It didn't exist, so we built it.

Rare Company brings together carefully selected owners with real businesses, real numbers and something meaningful at stake. No spectators or professional networkers, and nobody admitted simply to fill a seat.

Just capable owners, meeting regularly, telling each other the truth and helping one another make better decisions about what they earn, what they own and what they eventually walk away with.

Because the people around you shape the decisions you make. And the decisions you make shape what your business becomes and what goals you eventually achieve.
