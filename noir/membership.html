Part two

Your Core Circle

Monthly · In your city

This group will change your business and personal life, forever.

What makes Rare Company different is the members. Ours have major wins behind them and are still aggressive about growth, in business and in life.

Your Circle holds eight to ten established owners in your city, meeting in person every month. Every session is led by an exited founder, with experts and specialists brought in as the challenges demand.

What happens at a session

Around three hours, scheduled to suit the majority of your Circle. This is the group you get close with - there for you as much as you are for them. You'll chew the fat, go deep and work out solutions to the biggest challenges around the table.

Every session broadly follows the same shape:

  • Business review
  • Individual net worth review
  • A deep dive into a shared challenge
  • Accountability and support into the next month

And every member at every Circle commits to the same terms: what's said stays in the room, real numbers go on the table, and support is specific enough to act on. That's how we grow together.

How we build one

Every seat is interviewed, vetted and there by application - never a direct competitor.

  • We don't match by industry. Ten people with the same problems reach the same answers - fresh angles win.
  • We don't sort by turnover alone. Trajectory, energy and lived experience count for more than this year's number.
  • We don't build a room of people just like you. We match a ladder, not a mirror: peers at your level, owners a few years ahead, and founders who have finished the journey - so someone at the table has already faced what's coming next for you.

What this is not

  • A traditional networking session
  • A place to sell or prospect
  • A group of everyone in the same boat
  • Theory, or support you can't act on

Part three

Your New Network

Every member · Slack · On demand

The power of every member in the country on your side.

Between Circle sessions, the Rare Network supports you on demand. The whole membership sits in one private Slack, with channels for the company, the money and the rest of life - and our team of specialists on call whenever you need them.

Post your challenge at midnight and the answers come within hours. Real ones, from owners who have already solved it - not AI slop, and not well-meaning opinions from people who have never carried the weight. It's how one member got hands-on advice on a £12 million acquisition offer within 24 hours of the approach.
